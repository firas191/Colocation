# Sources of the p7_photos v1 photos

The photos are not in this repository. `scripts/windows/p4.ps1 -Step photos-fetch` downloads them (1,280 px wide copies)
from Wikimedia Commons into `..\photo_bench`, and `scripts/photos.py upload` refuses a file whose SHA-256 differs from the
one recorded in `p7_photos_v1.jsonl`. Each photo is used under the licence below; the author is as given on its Commons page.
