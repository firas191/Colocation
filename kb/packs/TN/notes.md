# TN pack: what is contradictory or unknown

Leads only. Nothing here is a finding until the sources are fetched and read.
Each line names the sources that disagree; the evaluation's contradiction
queries are built from these after the texts are read.

- Lease registration (enregistrement du bail): `tn-notaire-tunisienumerique` (no registration
  needed for residential leases) against `tn-mehat-logement-locatif-2014` and `tn-diwan-location`
  (registration within 60 days) and `tn-web6-droits-locataire` (recommended).
- Registration fee: 5 TND per page in `tn-profiscal-det-ch7-2006` (2006) against 20 TND per page in
  `tn-mehat-logement-locatif-2014` (2014); the primary text is `tn-cdet-2017`, itself possibly outdated.
- Sub-letting and colocation consent: `tn-cyriljarnias-location` (written consent) against what the
  COC articles on sub-letting say (to read).
- Law 76-35 applies only to older buildings (`tn-mehat-logement-locatif-2014`); guides that cite it
  generally may be wrong for most current flatshares.
